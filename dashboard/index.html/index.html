How a price is made

implied = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity

  • Multiple. For each category, the median market-cap / TVL of the protocols that already launched, winsorised to [0.01, 5] and shrunk toward the global median with 8 pseudo-rows of weight, so a thin category borrows the market's evidence instead of inventing its own.
  • Momentum. 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +100%) × 0.5. Unreported change is neutral.
  • Maturity. 0.5 + 0.5 × √(age / 4 years, capped). A protocol with no listing date is read as 365 days old.
  • Launch prior. The share of a category's protocols that already record a token, shrunk the same way. Context for the farm plan; it never moves the price.

Fitted from this snapshot alone: Nothing is hardcoded per protocol, and wrapped, staked or bridged products of tokens that already trade are kept off the board.
