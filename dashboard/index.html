How a price is made

implied = TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity

  • Range, not a point. Each category's multiple is the median of its launched peers' market-cap / TVL, winsorised to [0.01, 5] and shrunk toward the global median with 8 pseudo-rows. The board also quotes the peer quartiles as a low–high range: the middle half of peers is what a launch plausibly prices inside.
  • Momentum. 1 + 0.6 × tanh(7-day change / 40), bounded so one freak week (this snapshot runs to +4,448%) cannot mint a top row. Unreported change is neutral.
  • Maturity. 0.45 + 0.55 × √(age / 5 years, capped). No listing date is read as 365 days, stated openly.
  • Conviction. A separate 0–100 farm score: launch prior 40, TVL scale 20, momentum 15, age in the prime window 15, chain breadth 10. It never moves the price.

How a price is made — the full method

Every figure on this board re-derives from the sealed snapshot and the formulas below. No protocol name is special-cased; no multiple is hardcoded.

1 · The peer evidence

The snapshot's 416 token-bearing protocols are the launches that already happened. For the 128 with both a market cap and a TVL, the raw multiple is mcap / TVL, winsorised into [0.01, 5] — outside that band a ratio measures TVL the token's cap does not belong to (one peer sits at 400x because its token trades against activity counted elsewhere), not a launch price.

2 · Category multiples, with a range

Each category's ratios are summarised by their first quartile, median and third quartile, and each statistic is shrunk toward the matching global one with 8 pseudo-rows of weight: fitted = (n × own + 8 × global) / (n + 8). A category with no usable ratio takes the global fit outright. The median prices the central value; the quartiles bound the range the board quotes — the honest answer to peers that price anywhere from 0.05x to 1.06x inside the middle half.

3 · Momentum that a freak week cannot bend

momentum = 1 + 0.6 × tanh(change7d / 40), bounded in (0.4, 1.6). This snapshot's 7-day changes run from −73% to +4,448% with a median of −8%: a linear factor would hand the top of the board to one inflow spike. The tanh curve is near-linear for ordinary weeks and flat for freak ones. An unreported change scores a neutral 1.

4 · Maturity

maturity = 0.45 + 0.55 × √(min(age, 1825 days) / 1825). A protocol earns value for having run with no token; the curve is steepest early, when survival is being proven. A protocol with no listing date is read as 365 days old — a stated default, flagged on its card, never hidden.

5 · Conviction, kept apart from value

Value says what a launch could be worth; conviction (0–100) says how good a farm the row is: the category's fitted launch prior × 40, TVL scale toward $250M (square-rooted) × 20, the clamped 7-day change × 15, age inside the 180–1460-day prime window × 15, and chain breadth toward 8 chains × 10. Grades run A (70+), B (55+), C (40+), D below. The detail view shows every part, so a grade can be argued with.

6 · Who is kept off the board, and why

Token-bearing protocols never appear: they already launched. Neither do wrapped, staked or bridged products of a token that trades, versioned names, or an exchange's own TVL record — the classes that keep WBTC, Binance staked ETH and USDT0 off a board of future launches. What remains is 198 candidates, the 40 watchlist protocols among them, ranked by central implied value with ties broken on TVL then slug, so the board is a pure function of the snapshot.
